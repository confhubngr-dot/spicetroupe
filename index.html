Tailored to your vision

Every event tells a story. We are here to tell yours.

Every Spice Troupe production is designed around your vision. We go beyond performance to create bespoke cultural experiences that captivate your audience and elevate your event.

Our productions combine breathtaking costume, live percussion, cultural processions, flag displays, theatrical choreography and immersive storytelling to showcase the richness of Africa and cultures from around the world. More than performers, we are cultural ambassadors — committed to preserving heritage, fostering cultural appreciation, and creating experiences that resonate long after the final performance.

  • Costume, choreography and music included in the quoted price
  • Sets from 6 to 30 minutes, or a full evening in three appearances
  • Live drummers and vocalists available for every traditional production
  • We travel with our own PA, and can run off your generator

The repertoire

Six kinds of production.

Traditional, contemporary and theatrical work celebrating cultures from Africa and around the world. Each carries its own music, costume and staging requirements.

Traditional African

Indigenous cultural performance

Danced by performers who grew up in these traditions, with live percussion rather than a recorded track.

Pan-African & Afro Fusion

Contemporary, rooted

Original productions drawing on the rhythms and movement vocabulary of the continent, built for audiences who want something current without losing the heritage underneath it.

Contemporary & Ballet

Expressive and elegant

Modern choreography for premium events and stage productions.

Carnival & Parade

High-energy spectacle

Vibrant costume, cultural processions and dynamic choreography, on a stage or moving through a venue.

Global Cultural Dances

Cultures across the globe

Performances inspired by traditions from around the world, for international audiences, embassy evenings and events where the room is not only Nigerian.

Theatrical & Flag Choreography

Story-driven production

Narrative productions for opening ceremonies, conferences, galas and international events, combining choreography, flag displays and staging into one piece.

Booking

Four steps, about ten minutes of your time.

Step 1

Send us the date

Venue, start time, indoor or outdoor, and roughly how many guests. WhatsApp is fine — four lines is plenty.

Step 2

We propose a set

Two or three options with running times, dancer numbers and a fixed price. Nothing is held until you say yes.

Step 3

Payment secures the date

Only payment confirms a reservation. Booking months ahead? A 70% deposit locks in current rates. You get a written agreement and a music brief.

Step 4

We arrive early

Dancers arrive two hours before the set to change, check the floor and meet whoever is running the room.
