Event types

Six kinds of day we know well.

Traditional weddings

An entrance for the couple, a set matched to both families’ traditions, and a section that brings guests onto the floor. We work with your planner directly on timings.

White weddings & receptions

A shorter, tighter set after the meal, then a first dance if you’ve commissioned one. Fits venues where the floor is smaller than the guest list suggests.

Corporate & product launches

Opening numbers, interval sets and stage-managed entrances. We liaise with your AV supplier and can supply a technical rider.

Festivals & carnivals

Repeat sets across a day, outdoor stages and parade routes. Priced by the day rather than by the set.

Cultural days & receptions

State cultural events, embassy evenings, association galas and diaspora functions. Tell us which traditions the room will want to see and we’ll build to that.

Schools & youth programmes

Assembly performances and workshops for up to 60 pupils at a time, with a scheme of work you can keep.

Add-ons

Things people ask for once they’ve booked.

  • Live drummers — bátá or ogene ensemble rather than a recorded track
  • Parade entrance — dancers lead the couple or guests in, in full costume
  • Guest workshop — 30 minutes, up to 40 people, no experience needed
  • Filmed set — a videographer we work with, edited and sent within a fortnight
  • Costume for the couple — sourced and fitted for the entrance or first dance

Practical

Questions we get every week.

How far in advance should we book?

December and the carnival season fill up by August. The rest of the year, three to six weeks is usually enough, and we can sometimes take a date with a fortnight’s notice. Commissioned pieces need six weeks whatever the date.

What happens if it rains?

Outdoor sets need a dry, level surface. If it’s wet we move indoors or shorten to the styles that are safe on what’s available — we won’t cancel on you, and we won’t dance on a floor where someone will get hurt.

Do you travel outside Lagos?

Yes, anywhere in the country. Transport and accommodation for the company are added to the quote, and for far dates we usually travel the day before rather than risk the road on the morning of your event.

Do you need a stage?

No. A cleared floor of 5m × 4m is enough for most sets. Send the dimensions or a photo and we’ll tell you honestly what fits.

Can we choose the music?

Yes, within reason. Send tracks at least three weeks ahead so the choreography can be rebuilt around them. The traditional sets have their own music and drumming, which we won’t swap for a recording.

Tell us the date and we’ll tell you what fits.

Send the venue, the time of day and the kind of crowd. You’ll get a reply with two or three options and a price the same day.
