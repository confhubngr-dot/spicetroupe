Terms

What the quote covers.

Worth reading before you confirm. Nothing here appears later as a surprise.

  1. All prices are quoted in Nigerian Naira. For international clients, a USD equivalent can be provided at the prevailing exchange rate at the time the quotation is issued.
  2. Costumes and logistics are added to the final invoice, calculated once the venue, scale and date are confirmed.
  3. A welfare fee applies to all bookings outside Lagos and Abuja.
  4. Where flights and accommodation are required, Spice Troupe arranges all travel and hotel bookings to maintain our service standards. Related costs are borne by the client.
  5. Requests for special costumes and props attract additional charges.
  6. Only payment confirms and secures your event date reservation.
  7. For events scheduled several months ahead, prices are subject to review based on prevailing economic conditions. Payment of 70% deposit locks in current rates for your date.

Practical

Questions we get every week.

How far in advance should we book?

December and the carnival season fill up by August. The rest of the year, three to six weeks is usually enough. Booking several months ahead is welcome — a 70% deposit locks in current rates, which protects you if prices are reviewed before your date.

How do we secure the date?

Payment confirms the reservation. Until payment reaches us, the date stays open to other enquiries, however far the conversation has gone.

What happens if it rains?

Outdoor sets need a dry, level surface. If it’s wet we move indoors or shorten to the styles that are safe on what’s available — we won’t cancel on you, and we won’t dance on a floor where someone will get hurt.

Do you travel outside Lagos and Abuja?

Yes, anywhere in the country and internationally. Bookings outside Lagos and Abuja carry a welfare fee, and where flights and hotels are needed we arrange them ourselves so the coordination holds together — those costs are borne by the client and itemised in the quote.

What is not included in the base fee?

Costumes and logistics. Both are calculated once the venue, scale and date are confirmed, then added to the final invoice. Special costumes and props requested for a particular theme are charged separately again.

Do you need a stage?

No. A cleared floor of 5m × 4m is enough for most sets. Send the dimensions or a photo and we’ll tell you honestly what fits.

Can we choose the music?

Yes, within reason. Send tracks at least three weeks ahead so the choreography can be rebuilt around them. The traditional sets have their own music and drumming, which we won’t swap for a recording.

Tell us the date and the event type.

Send the venue, the time of day and the kind of crowd. You’ll get a reply with two or three options and a price the same day.
